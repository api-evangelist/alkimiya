> distributes clinical decision support systems grounded in Evidence Based Medicine.
> It is the exclusive Italian distributor of EBMEDS (a product of Duodecim Publication

## Products

- [EBMEDS](https://www.alkimiya.eu/soluzioni/ebmeds/283/): third-generation clinical
  decision support system owned by Duodecim Publication Company and distributed
  exclusively in Italy by Alkimiya. Provides diagnostic and therapeutic suggestions,
  alerts and warnings, drug-interaction and contraindication checking, dosage warnings
  and medication reconciliation. Over 1,000 clinical algorithms written in server-side
  JavaScript (ECMAScript 5). Any EBMEDS API belongs to Duodecim, not to Alkimiya.
- [SmartCare](https://www.alkimiya.eu/soluzioni/smartcare/427/): Alkimiya's own platform
  under development for proactive support of post-hospitalization and chronic patients —
  a Digital Clinical Decision Pathway knowledge base, a rules engine and an integration
  API. Enables differential diagnosis, assisted history-taking and examination, shared
  decision making, order sets, assisted data capture and patient empowerment.
- [Continuum of Care](https://www.alkimiya.eu/progetti-innovativi/715/): a SaaS platform
  and smartphone apps for interactive care plans, driven by an Evidence Based Pathway
  engine, with a services marketplace for care-pathway providers. Operational site in
  Massafra (TA).
